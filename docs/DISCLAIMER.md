# Game ownership and compatibility disclaimer

[Back to overview](../README.md)

**You must own a legally obtained copy of the PC version of Devil May Cry 3:
Special Edition and supply your own game files.**

The required executable is named **`dmc3se.exe`**. The complete matching game
folder must include `GDATA.AFS`, `native/rom`, and `native/sound`. The executable
name alone is insufficient: the launcher verifies this exact SHA-256 target:

```text
49f80df5cce75d2a2b942f25c8ebe6ac6aceac87e7869c8e35073a96397cf8f4
```

The HD Collection executable and other executable revisions are not substitutes
for that target. This repository and its releases do not provide the original
EXE, game archives, gameplay textures/models, audio, movies, or save data.
The app's logo is UI branding; the screenshots are documentation only.

Capcom and their respective rights holders. This is an independent experimental
project, not an official, endorsed, or affiliated Capcom release.

made available under that license. It does not grant a license to the original
game, Capcom's branding/artwork/screenshots, or third-party components under
their own terms. See [third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY.md).

This is a development preview supplied without warranty. Full campaign
compatibility and a fixed frame rate have not been established. Keep backups
of your own saves before testing updates. See [Status](STATUS.md) for the
available evidence and known limitations.
